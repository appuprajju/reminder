Privacy Policy for reminderme

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Website: https://reminder.makeuperfect.in · Developer: Prajwal M (</yourstudio>) · Contact: appuprajju807@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how reminderme (“we,” “our,” or “the App”), accessible at https://reminder.makeuperfect.in and through our mobile/PWA packages, collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes your information when you create alarms, daily reminders, voice reminders, and optional Google Workspace integrations.

1. Information We Collect

  • On-Device Reminder & Preference Data (Guest Mode): If you use reminderme without signing in, all reminders, alarms, checklists, schedules, ringtone choices, and clock preferences remain stored locally on your device using browser/device local storage and IndexedDB.
  • Account Information (Optional Sign-In): If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or Email & Password via Firebase Authentication, we collect your name, email address, unique user identifier (UID), optional profile photo URL, and timezone so your reminders stay synchronized across your devices.
  • Synced Reminders & Schedules: When signed in, your reminders, alarm times, recurrence schedules, completion status, and notes are stored in your private Firebase Cloud Firestore collection scoped exclusively to your account.
  • Voice Capture & Assistant Commands (Optional): When you tap a Voice, Hey Siri, Hey Google, or Hands-Free button, speech recognition runs through your device’s built-in browser/OS speech engine to convert your spoken words into a reminder title and time. reminderme does not record, upload, or store raw microphone audio on our servers.
  • Notification & Device Permissions (Optional): With your permission, we use OS notification permissions, exact alarm scheduling, push subscription endpoints, and vibration solely to trigger your scheduled reminders and alarms on time.

2. Google User Data & Google Workspace API Usage

Basic Google Sign-In only requests your standard profile name, email address, and profile photo to authenticate your account. Separately, if you explicitly choose to connect Google Calendar and Gmail inside reminderme, the App requests access to the following Google API scopes strictly for user-facing productivity features:

  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events): Used solely to display your upcoming Google Calendar events alongside your reminders in the Today and Schedule views, and to create a Google Calendar event from a reminder only when you explicitly confirm the event preview dialog.
  • Gmail Read-Only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): Used solely to fetch recent primary inbox message metadata (sender, subject, date, and short snippet) directly in your browser so you can turn an email into a reminder with one tap. We do not store your Gmail messages on external servers.
  • Gmail Send (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send): Used solely when you explicitly click and confirm sending a reminder alert or daily agenda digest email to your own email address. reminderme never sends emails automatically or without your explicit confirmation.
Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure:
reminderme’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Strict Prohibitions on Google User Data

  • We never sell, rent, or broker Google user data or personal information to third parties.
  • We never use Google Calendar or Gmail data for targeted advertising, marketing profiles, or credit/lending decisions.
  • We never use Google Workspace API data to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Google OAuth access tokens are held strictly in active session memory in your browser and are cleared automatically on sign-out or page reload; they are never persisted in localStorage or sessionStorage.

3. How We Store & Protect Your Data

  • All communications between your device, Firebase services, and Google APIs are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Cloud-synced reminders in Firebase Firestore are protected by strict user-ownership security rules so only your authenticated account can read or write your records.
  • Google Calendar and Gmail API requests are made directly from your authenticated client session using short-lived OAuth tokens.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. We rely only on essential infrastructure providers required to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure account authentication and private cross-device reminder sync.
  • Google Workspace APIs (Optional): Only when you explicitly authorize Google Calendar or Gmail access.

5. Data Retention, Revoking Access & Account Deletion

  • Edit or Delete Reminders Anytime: You can delete individual reminders, export a JSON backup, or sign out directly inside the App’s Today and Profile tabs.
  • Revoke Google Workspace Access Anytime: You can disconnect reminderme from your Google Account at any time via https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Full Account & Data Deletion: To request permanent deletion of your account and all cloud-synced reminder data, email appuprajju807@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Delete reminderme Account.” All cloud records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

6. Children’s Privacy

reminderme is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect feature improvements or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
